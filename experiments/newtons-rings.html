EXPERIMENT 02

Newton’s Rings: Radius of Curvature of a Plano-Convex Lens

Study interference in reflected light and determine the radius of curvature of a plano-convex lens from ring diameters.

Learning objectives

Understand thin-film interference, measure ring diameters and use the Newton’s rings relation for radius of curvature.
