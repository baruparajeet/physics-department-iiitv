What you are doing

In reflected light, interference between the reflected waves from the thin air film produces concentric bright and dark rings. The setup uses a sodium source, an inclined G1 plate, a plano-convex lens and a travelling microscope. The virtual apparatus is interactive: source intensity changes ring visibility, wavelength and lens radius change ring spacing, focusing changes sharpness, and microscope movement changes the ring position relative to the cross-wire.

Dark rings: Dn2 = 4nλRRadius: R = (Dn+m2 − Dn2) / (4mλ)
