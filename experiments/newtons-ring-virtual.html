What you are doing

In reflected light, coherent rays reflected from the lower surface of the plano-convex lens and the glass plate interfere and form concentric bright and dark rings. The source is a nearly monochromatic sodium source and the air film lies between the lens and optically flat plate. The manual procedure requires levelling the traveling microscope, focusing on the air film, adjusting G1 for maximum visibility, and measuring ring positions on both sides.

Dark rings: Dn2 = 4nλRRadius: R = (Dn+m2 − Dn2) / (4mλ)
