What you are doing

For normal incidence, the reflected rays from the lower surface of the plano-convex lens and the glass plate interfere in the thin air film. The result is a system of concentric bright and dark rings. The practical records distinct bright rings using a sodium source and a travelling microscope.

Bright rings: Dn2 = 4(n + 1/2)λRWorking formula: R = (Dn+m2 − Dn2) / (4mλ)

The simulator follows the manual's m = 5 pairing: (10,5), (9,4), (8,3), (7,2), and (6,1).
