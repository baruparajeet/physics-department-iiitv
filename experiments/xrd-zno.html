EXPERIMENT 10

XRD Pattern of ZnO Particles (Hexagonal)

Study the powder X-ray diffraction pattern of ZnO and identify the characteristic reflections of the hexagonal wurtzite structure.

Learning objectives

Apply Bragg’s law, index diffraction peaks using Miller indices and estimate lattice parameters from XRD data.
